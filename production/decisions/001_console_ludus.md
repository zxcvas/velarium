# 001 — Console ludus slice

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Decision
First playable demo stays a **C# console** game. Player is a **lanista** (school first). Hosting *munera* unlocks later. Antique framing; Roman names.

## Why
- Engine choice was blocking all systems work.
- A lanista's loop (roster, upkeep, rental, death-as-asset-loss) is the fantasy; editing games is the promotion.
- Console keeps the slice honest: if the loop is dull, graphics will not save it.

## Consequences
- No Godot / art track until M1 has been played and the loop holds.
- Currency in *denarii*. Dates in the Roman civil calendar (a.u.c.).
- Economy follows the jurist Gaius in spirit: small *pro sudore* pay, large compensation if the rented man is killed or maimed.
