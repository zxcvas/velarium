Fact sheet

Train gladiators, feed the house, rent them to magistrates — if the school earns fama, you may stage a munus yourself.

Boilerplate: Amphiteater is a turn-based ludus management game. The player is a lanista in Capua in a.u.c. DCCLXXXII (29 CE). Presentation is NES/SNES pixel with cartoon KO. The GitHub repository is named Velarium — the awning, not the product lock.

  • Genre Turn-based ludus / familia management
